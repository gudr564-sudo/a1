Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

1. Overview

AURÉA ("we", "us") is a demonstration storefront. This policy explains what limited data is handled when you browse this site.

2. Information we collect

  • No personal information is collected directly by us. There are no accounts, sign-ups, or real payments.
  • Usage data (pages visited, approximate location, device type) is collected by Google Analytics as described below.
  • Shopping bag data (items, sizes, quantities) is stored only in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

3. Cookies

This site uses Google Analytics (gtag.js), which sets cookies to measure anonymous usage statistics such as page views and session duration. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

4. Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, photography from Unsplash's content delivery network, and analytics scripts from Google Analytics. When your browser requests these files, those providers may see standard request data (such as your IP address) as described in their own privacy policies.

5. Children's privacy

This site is a design demo and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@learnvista.cc.
